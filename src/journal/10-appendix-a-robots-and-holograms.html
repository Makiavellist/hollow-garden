Hard-Light Actor

A hologram given mass and edge by a projected force field: the Garden’s stock villain, henchman and ghost. Celmak dresses each actor in a costume for the scene.

Hologram subtype. A hologram is a construct of solid light. It is immune to critical hits and precision damage. It is emitter-bound and subject to shadow disruption (below).

HARD-LIGHT ACTOR — CR 12

XP 19,200 · N Medium construct (hologram)

Init +7; Senses darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +20

DEFENSE

AC 27, touch 21, flat-footed 24 (+8 deflection, +3 Dex, +6 natural)

hp 108 (16d10+20); fast healing 10

Fort +5, Ref +8, Will +6

Defensive Abilities hard-light body; Immune construct traits, critical hits, precision damage

Weaknesses emitter-bound, shadow disruption

OFFENSE

Speed 40 ft., fly 40 ft. (perfect)

Melee 2 hard-light blades +21 (2d8+4 force)

Ranged light lance +19 touch (6d6 fire)

STATISTICS

Str 18, Dex 16, Con —, Int 10, Wis 12, Cha 14

Base Atk +16; CMB +20; CMD 41

Feats Combat Reflexes, Improved Initiative, Lunge, Point-Blank Shot, Power Attack, Precise Shot, Vital Strike, Weapon Focus (hard-light blade)

Skills Acrobatics +19, Perception +20

Languages none; it speaks scripted lines in any language Celmak knows

SQ costume

SPECIAL ABILITIES

Costume (Ex) The actor’s appearance is set for each scene. Suggested costumes: Swarm warriors (Episode 3), Casandalee’s past selves (The 113th Take), Lords of Rust thugs, or the party’s own enemies from earlier adventures. A costume is solid light, so disbelieving it does nothing.

Emitter-Bound (Ex) The actor exists only within 120 feet of a working holographic emitter. Each scene has 1d4+2 hidden emitters (Perception DC 25 to spot; hardness 10, 30 hp). With no emitter in range, the actor winks out until one is restored. Its fast healing works only while an emitter is in range.

Hard-Light Body (Ex) The actor’s blades and lance affect incorporeal creatures normally, and its blades deal force damage.

Light Lance (Ex) This integrated laser has a range increment of 60 feet. It resolves as a touch attack, passes through force effects without harming them, and cannot harm invisible creatures.

Shadow Disruption (Ex) In dim light, the actor’s attacks have a 20% miss chance and it loses fast healing. In darkness, including magical darkness or a fetchling’s shadow ability, it winks out until light returns: it cannot act or be targeted. A darkness effect centred on an emitter shuts that emitter down for the duration.

Holographic Double (template)

Celmak builds a double of a creature from its Muse scan, then sets it loose to hunt the original. Doubles appear only in the Season Finale.

CR The original’s level or CR −2 (CR 11 for a 13th-level PC).

Building a double. Start from the original’s character sheet as it was at the Muse scan. Change it as follows:

  • Type: construct (hologram), with emitter-bound, hard-light body and shadow disruption as the Hard-Light Actor.
  • Hit points: half the original’s maximum. It has no Constitution score.
  • Defences and offence: the original’s AC, saves, attack bonuses, damage, feats, skills and class features. It is immune to mind-affecting effects in addition to the hologram immunities.
  • Resources: half the original’s spell slots, spells known and daily uses, rounded down. It has none of the original’s consumables, and activated or charged items do not work for it. Passive item bonuses are copied.
  • Fear knowledge (Ex): once per round as a swift action, the double taunts its original with the fear from its scan. The original must succeed at a DC 24 Will save or be shaken for 1 round (emotion, mind-affecting).

Quick double. If you lack time to rebuild a sheet, use the original’s attack bonuses and save DCs −2, half its hit points, and its three most-used abilities.
