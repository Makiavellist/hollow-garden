ANCHOR ARRAY SCHEMATIC

Price 3,000 gp; Slot none; Weight —; Capacity —; Usage — A data spike holding the Lock Tree’s engineering drawings. Plugged into any reader, it grants a +5 bonus on checks to find, disable or understand the Dimensional Anchor Array. Afterward, it grants a +2 bonus on checks against any Androffan dimensional anchor, which may matter at Silver Mount.

AMBASSADOR’S RECORDING CRYSTAL

Price 4,000 gp; Slot none; Weight —; Capacity —; Usage — A palm-sized Androffan memory crystal that plays back, in light and sound, the evening the kasatha ambassadors spent on the entertainment deck. Once per day, a kasatha who spends a minute watching it gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and Will saves for 1 hour.

DIVINITY ENTERTAINMENT ARCHIVE

Price priceless; Slot none; Weight —; Capacity —; Usage — A data spike holding the entertainment deck’s records, partial crew logs and Celmak’s footage of every contestant. Its owner gains a +4 bonus on Knowledge checks about the Divinity, Silver Mount and Androffans. It also holds one secret of your choice, such as a maintenance route into Silver Mount, crew lists, or logs from before Unity went mad.

HARD-LIGHT EMITTER BRACER

Price 16,000 gp; Slot wrist; Weight 1 lb.; Capacity 10; Usage 3 charges per use As a move action, the wearer projects either a hard-light shield, granting a +4 shield bonus to AC (a force effect), or a hard-light blade that functions as a +2 short sword dealing force damage. Either lasts 1 minute. Darkness suppresses the effect while the wearer stands in it. Construction Requirements Craft Technological Arms and Armor, military lab; Cost 8,000 gp; Craft DC 26.

ANDROID SERVICE CRADLE

Price 12,000 gp; Slot none; Weight 8 lb.; Capacity 10; Usage 5 charges per use A folding maintenance cradle from the Divinity’s android workshops, “complimentary with purchase.” An android or other construct that rests in it for 10 minutes regains 10d6 hit points and is cured of one of the following conditions: fatigued, sickened, staggered or confused. It also lets its user find and purge factory override phrases, like the one Celmak holds over Meyanda, without a check. Construction Requirements Craft Technological Item, military lab; Cost 6,000 gp; Craft DC 26.

LEDGER OF THE FALLEN

Price 2,000 gp; Slot none; Weight 1 lb.; Capacity —; Usage — A datapad listing every adventurer who died in the Garden, with their gear, their home towns and their heirs. Its holder gains a +4 bonus on Diplomacy checks with anyone named in it or related to someone who is. Once, it identifies the original owner of one item in the hoard of the fallen.

MONOFILAMENT FAN

Price 4,000 gp; Type exotic light melee weapon; Damage 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium); Critical 18–20/×2;

Damage type slashing; Weight 1 lb. A folding fan whose ribs carry monofilament edges. It can be used with Weapon Finesse and ignores the first 10 points of an object’s hardness. A creature wielding one in each hand counts as wielding a light weapon in its off hand.

MUSE VISOR

Price 26,000 gp; Slot head; Weight 1 lb.; Capacity 10; Usage 1 charge per scan Celmak’s own mind-scanning optics, salvaged from her body. The party gets it only if Celmak is destroyed, surrenders, or hands it over as part of a deal. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on Sense Motive checks. As a standard action, the wearer can scan one creature within 60 feet, as detect thoughts with all three rounds of information gained at once (Will DC 20 negates). Construction Requirements Craft Technological Item, military lab; Cost 13,000 gp; Craft DC 28.

NANITE RESONANCE LATTICE

Price 28,000 gp; Slot neck; Weight —; Capacity 10; Usage 2 charges per pulse A mesh of silver filaments that settles into the skin of the throat. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on Knowledge (engineering) checks and a +2 bonus on caster level checks against constructs. When the wearer casts a spell or uses a nanite-based ability, they can spend a swift action to emit an override pulse: every robot within 30 feet must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be staggered for 1 round. A wearer with the nanite bloodline treats the lattice’s save DC as 22. Construction Requirements Craft Technological Item, military lab; Cost 14,000 gp; Craft DC 28.

SEED OF THE HOLLOW GARDEN

Aura moderate conjuration; CL 13th; Slot none; Price 25,000 gp; Weight — A living acorn grown from the awakened Lock Tree. While carried, it grants a +2 insight bonus on saves against illusions: its bearer can feel what is real. Planted in the Hollow Garden’s dome after the Core is shut down, it turns the synthetic garden into a living fey glade over a month. Planted anywhere else, it grows overnight into a 200-foot-wide grove where the bearer and their allies can always rest undisturbed, as private sanctum. Once planted, it is used up. Construction Requirements none; it grows only from the Lock Tree.

TEMPORAL REWIND CARTRIDGE

Price 9,000 gp; Slot none; Weight —; Capacity 3; Usage 1 charge per use A cartridge from the holographic bridge’s captain’s chair that should not exist yet. As an immediate action after rolling a d20, but before the result is announced, the holder can reroll and take the better result. A kasatha raised by a time traveller may use it without the charge being spent once per day: it simply remembers how. Construction Requirements none; its origin is unknown.
