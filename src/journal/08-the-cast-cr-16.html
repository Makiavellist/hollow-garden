Vlathherex’s demons are a CR 16 fight if the party crosses them, but they are worth more as uneasy allies: they want the dimensional lock down as badly as the party does. Celmak treats them as “regular cast” and sends them into scenes when the ratings sag.

The Cast Quarters

A ransacked dressing room. Mirrors are smashed, costumes slashed, and someone has carved Abyssal obscenities into a makeup table. A huge four-armed shape lounges on a pile of couches, flicking through holographic channels with a pincer.

The demons fled the Worldwound through the failed Wardstone border and found what looked like a perfect hiding place. They have been stuck for months. They are bored, restless and increasingly afraid that Celmak never means to let them leave.

Negotiating with Vlathherex

Vlathherex starts indifferent. A DC 20 Diplomacy check makes him friendly: he agrees to an alliance on his terms. A DC 30 check makes him helpful: he agrees on the party’s terms. The Merchant gains a +4 bonus if they outbid him for the schematic or share it with him, and the cast’s contract (Lot 10) grants its own bonus.

What he wants. He wants the dimensional lock shut down, a promise that neither side strikes until both are outside, and a fair share of the hoard of the fallen.

What he offers.

  • Information. The demons have seen the Booth and the Core Vault behind it. They know Celmak keeps two spare stand-ins charging there, and that the Celmak in the Booth “never smells of anything.” Old Gutter adds, unprompted, that the real one smells of lake water.
  • Muscle. In the finale, the cast fights the Doppel-cast, removing two doubles of the party’s choice and reducing the encounter to CR 16.
  • Distraction. The cast can tie up Celmak’s drones while the party works on the Lock Tree, removing the Topiary Hounds from Episode 4.

Will he keep his word? He is a demon. When the lock falls, he keeps his bargain only if he is magically bound (lesser geas, mark of justice or similar) or if betrayal looks more dangerous than honesty. Otherwise, he tries to charm a companion, grab whatever loot is close, and greater teleport out with Shaye and Murrow. Ozzrikar stays as long as it has a wish to grant.

Old Gutter stays out of spite.

If it comes to a fight

The party might attack the Cast Quarters, or Celmak might send the cast into a scene as “special guest stars” after a betrayal. Either way, all five demons fight together.

Tactics. Old Gutter opens with unholy blight and its unholy nimbus while Ozzrikar casts confusion and reverse gravity. Shaye and Murrow charm and kiss the companions, especially anyone Celmak’s scan says is lonely. Vlathherex flies over the line and power-attacks the weakest spellcaster, using Dazzling Display and Shatter Defenses. None of them can teleport while the lock holds, and they know it; they fight to the death because they have nowhere to run.
